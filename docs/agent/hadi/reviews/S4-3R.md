## Finding 1 (overrun order) reproduced

Run `stall0` (overrun handled after the drain) vs `stall1` (at the first delivered sample), stream `stall`, pass 0:

- `stall0`: `G 2920 15 1` (15 samples lost, flag set), then `O 2951 0`: the overrun is handled after the whole drain at index 2951 and reports `dropped = 0`. The window with trigger 2792 (start 2777, would end at 2926) was open when the gap arrived; the drain delivered samples 2920..2951, so capture filled the buffer across the gap and went READY at index 2941 (2926 + 15 missing). `capture_overrun()` then saw `CAPTURE_READY`, not `CAPTURING`, and returned false (capture.c: `dropped = (s_state == CAPTURE_CAPTURING)`), so the window with a 15-sample hole was classified (`W 2777 2792 2941 2951 ... id 3 conf 58`). `python_check` reports it as one of the two "windows spanning a gap" (one per pass) whose C result differs from the contiguous Python window.
- `stall1`: `O 2920 1` before `G 2920 15 1`: handled before the first delivered sample, `capture_overrun()` sees CAPTURING, drops the window, `trigger_release` is called; no `W` line for it, `span.py build stall1` finds 0 spanning windows; `dropped=2` in the summary vs `dropped=1` in `stall0`, and the gesture appears as `gap` (22 hits) instead of a corrupt hit (23).
- The same mechanism explains why the effect is rare: it needs the gap to fall in the last <= 31 samples of an open window so the window completes in the same drain. The third stall of the `stall` stream was placed at `onset + 118` to hit exactly that. In the other 25 overruns per pass both orders behave identically. `stall2` (adds `trigger_resync`) is identical to `stall1` in every statistic.

## Finding 6 (conf and distance distribution) and alternatives

Confirmed: typical MLP confidence on hit windows is median 86 (correct windows only: 88.5), P90 139 in `big`; median 67, P90 119 in `main`; only 1 of 154 and 0 of 46 reach 192, so `GESTURE_OVERRIDE_CONF = 192` makes the MLP "weak" nearly always. `tight` is lower (median 29). Distances: other-class windows have nearest-template L1 median 976 (`big`) / 898 (`main`) but a tail down to 382 / 275 (12 of 118 below 600); same-class windows have median 792 / 488, and 29 of 34 are above 600. Radius 1200 admits 79 of 118 other-class windows, which matches the 78 overrides. Different template pairs give the same direction (main 41 %, big 48 %, stall 50 %, tight 28 % pass-1 accuracy), so the result does not hinge on one pair. Caveat: the model, the streams and the templates are all synthetic from one generator and one seed each.
